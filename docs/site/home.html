Overview

A complete, self-contained media center for your Wii

WiiMC-SS XE plays your video files, audio collection, radio streams, and physical DVDs directly off your SD card, USB hard drive, or disc drive — no transcoding server or PC required.

Paired with WiiFin-XE for the complete Wii media experience

WiiFin-XE streams your library from a remote Jellyfin server with a modern Netflix-style row interface. WiiMC-SS XE plays files directly from your physical media (SD card, USB hard drive, and DVDs).

With XE integration, you can switch seamlessly between both apps: jump to WiiFin from WiiMC's settings menu, and return directly to WiiFin when exiting from Local Media.

Learn more about our companion streaming app at WiiFin-XE.

Smooth Video Playback

True 720x480p tiled rendering, MPEG-2 interlaced display, 30 fps vsync in 480i/p modes, and optional .dash deblock skipping for fast H.264 playback.

Music & Embedded Cover Art

Plays MP3, MP4/AAC, ALAC, FLAC, OGG, WMA, AIFF, and looping game audio (ADX, BRSTM) with full embedded and folder cover art.

Online Radio Streams

Listen to SHOUTcast and internet radio streams with dynamic live cover art updates from TuneIn, Yggdrasil Radio, and ANISON.FM, plus automatic reconnect on dropouts.

DVD Playback

Direct DVD-Video playback with DVD menu navigation and full chapter/audio/subtitle selection on compatible Wii disc drives.

WiiFin-XE Bridge

Switch between WiiFin-XE and WiiMC-SS XE with one click. Automatic fail-safe verification checks device paths and DOL headers before switching.

Controller Support

Pick up a Wii Remote with pointer controls, Classic Controller, GameCube controller, or Nintendo 3DS for effortless couch navigation.

Install

On your Wii in three steps

You only need a softmodded Wii or vWii with the Homebrew Channel, and an SD card or USB storage drive.

  1. Copy to your SD card or USB drive

    Extract the release archive so that boot.dol, meta.xml, and icon.png are located in sd:/apps/wiimc/ (or usb:/apps/wiimc/).

  2. Launch from Homebrew Channel

    Open the Homebrew Channel, find WiiMC-SS XE, and press Start.

  3. Enjoy your media

    Browse your videos, audio folders, or DVDs with your favorite controller.
